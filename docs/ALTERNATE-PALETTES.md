# Alternate palette studies

These studies explore a different overall mood while preserving recognizable
functional groups. The accepted Amber Hinge + Rose keeper remains unchanged.
Solar Draft was saved as a selected physical trial using its own source-slot
export and strictly verified after Sony Hub reload. Physical feedback found it
enjoyable and balanced overall, while the white digits drew too much attention.
A refinement was requested; no separate all-legends readability confirmation was
recorded. The original palette and its identity remain unchanged.

Solar Draft tests a warm central writing field against a cool crown and a white
number rail. The green Win/Fn pair becomes an isolated landmark. The purpose is
an enjoyable alternate visual mood, not a claim that amber improves performance,
comfort or sleep. A preference for green in earlier comparisons does not decide
this separate question in advance.

| Functional group | Keys | Requested RGB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Letters, Space and ordinary punctuation | 35 | Amber `#FFB000` |
| Digits and both bracket keys | 12 | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Win/Fn hinge | 2 | Green `#00FF00` |
| F1–F4 | 4 | Violet `#4400FF` |
| F5–F8 | 4 | Azure `#00A0FF` |
| F9–F12 | 4 | Cyan `#00FFFF` |

Ordinary punctuation means minus/underscore, equals/plus, backslash/pipe,
semicolon/colon, quote, comma, period and slash/question mark. Each physical key
has one light for both shifted and unshifted legends. Together these groups
change exactly 61 keys relative to the keeper.

The remaining 20 keys retain their keeper colors: Tab/Caps/Enter violet
`#B000FF`, Ctrl blue-violet `#4400FF`, Alt magenta `#FF00E0`, Shift rose
`#FF0080`, tilde/arrows azure `#00A0FF`, Esc/Backspace/Delete red `#FF0000`,
Insert orange `#FF6A00` and Page Up/Page Down yellow `#FFE000`. Left/right modifier
pairs remain matched. All 81 keys remain illuminated at the requested 100%
brightness; that does not establish equal apparent brightness across hues.

This bounded revision changes only the ten physical digit keys `NUM_0` through
`NUM_9` from white `#FFFFFF` to the existing azure `#00A0FF`. Every other one of
Solar Draft's 71 colors stays exact: amber letters/Space/punctuation, white
brackets, the green Win/Fn pair, the cool function banks, modifiers and navigation
are all preserved. Its complete assignment still differs from the keeper on
61 keys and retains the same other 20 keeper colors. The Solar Draft original
remains separately available; this is a new study identity.

**Question:** does using the familiar azure hue make the digit row less dominant
while retaining useful grouping with the amber writing field?

In one physical trial, the amber letters led and the blue digits were readable,
meeting the bounded emphasis goal in that comparison. Whole-keyboard legend
readability and an overall ranking against other palettes were not established.
This records a perceptual response, not a measured brightness reduction. RGB
channel counts or screen appearance cannot predict the actual LED luminance or
attention a color receives. The ten-digit change held the rest of the composition
fixed so feedback could address that one relationship.

Azure digits may blend with F5–F8 and the other blue landmarks. Keeping the
brackets white may leave that pair too isolated or prominent. Those are risks to
observe, not reasons to change additional keys before this comparison. Assess
both digit readability and whether the amber writing field now receives the
wanted emphasis from the normal seated position.

## What would make it useful?

Ask whether the entire composition feels like an enjoyable alternate while key
legends and functional groups remain easy to recognize from typing distance.
Keep it as an alternate only after an actual person finds that combination useful.
It is a whole-composition study: a preference cannot establish the causal effect
of one changed key or one color.

The white digits' prominence was observed in Solar Draft's trial; Solar Azure
Rail addresses that bounded issue without revising the original. Other possible
risks remain unmeasured: red/orange stop and Insert keys merging with the amber
field, violet crown keys merging with the command frame, or any legends becoming
difficult to read. Screen RGB previews do not measure LED output, comfort,
attention or typing performance.

Preview and share it without changing hardware. The downloadable JSON is a
palette design, not a Sony native import. Any chosen physical trial still needs
the intended slot's fresh own export and backup, official Sony import/Save,
strict fresh-export verification and separate physical feedback. Never replace
the accepted keeper merely to test this alternative.
