# Punctuation studies

Three proposed variations of Amber Hinge + Rose. They change punctuation only; the green letters and Space, blue tilde, mint brackets, modifiers and F-row remain as accepted. None has been installed or physically evaluated.

| Preview | Changes | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Pale Quote](https://rana.github.io/inzone-profile-studio/?profile=amber-hinge-rose&colors=QUOTE.C0FFFF) | 1 physical key | One pale aqua quote key; every other green punctuation key stays green. |
| [Operator Ice](https://rana.github.io/inzone-profile-studio/?profile=amber-hinge-rose&colors=BACK_SLASH.FFFFFF%2CPLUS.FFFFFF%2CSLASH.FFFFFF%2CMINUS.FFFFFF) | 4 physical keys | Four white keys mark operators, paths and escapes. Comma, period, semicolon and quote stay green. |
| [Three-Role Split](https://rana.github.io/inzone-profile-studio/?profile=amber-hinge-rose&colors=SEMICOLON.A0FF00%2CQUOTE.00FFFF%2CCOMMA.A0FF00%2CBACK_SLASH.FFFFFF%2CPLUS.FFFFFF%2CSLASH.FFFFFF%2CPERIOD.A0FF00%2CMINUS.FFFFFF) | 8 physical keys | White operator/path keys, a cyan quote and chartreuse separators; the most exploratory study. |

Start with **Operator Ice**. It asks one bounded question: does one visibly separate punctuation group help when glancing down, without distracting from the green writing field? If it feels busy, Pale Quote is the smaller trial. Three-Role Split tests whether extra distinctions add anything useful. These are design hypotheses, not claims of faster typing.

## Exact assignments

**Pale Quote:** QUOTE #C0FFFF.

**Operator Ice:** MINUS #FFFFFF; PLUS #FFFFFF; SLASH #FFFFFF; BACK_SLASH #FFFFFF.

**Three-Role Split:** MINUS #FFFFFF; PLUS #FFFFFF; SLASH #FFFFFF; BACK_SLASH #FFFFFF; QUOTE #00FFFF; SEMICOLON #A0FF00; COMMA #A0FF00; PERIOD #A0FF00.

MINUS is the -/_ key, PLUS is the =/+ key, BACK_SLASH is the backslash/pipe key. Each physical key has one light: its shifted and unshifted legends cannot be colored separately. The roles are mnemonic groupings, not an exhaustive model of Go or Markdown syntax.

## Compare safely

In the studio, open **Explore studies** and choose a named study; it opens **Compare** automatically. Alternatively, open a preview link, then choose **Compare**. An opened variation link defaults to its accepted **Amber Hinge + Rose (original)** reference. The app labels these as local variations, never as newly accepted palettes. Reloading or sharing the URL reconstructs the exact RGB assignments and recognizes the exact study name. Temporary previews preserve existing saved variations; **Leave preview** returns to the previous palette. Opening a preview does not change a connected keyboard.

Screen RGB is not calibrated LED output. Pale aqua may look white; chartreuse may blend toward green or the warm function row. A real trial needs a chosen slot, its fresh source export, backup, official Sony import/Save, fresh export verification and the user's seated-distance feedback. Keep the accepted slot available for comparison.
