# Punctuation studies

Four punctuation variations of Amber Hinge + Rose. They change punctuation only; the green letters and Space, blue tilde, mint brackets, modifiers and F-row remain as accepted. Operator Ice was liked in a physical trial and chosen after an earlier comparison. No separate all-legends readability verdict was recorded. Three-Role Split was subsequently liked in a daylight trial: the separators were subtle but distinguishable and felt appropriately emphasized. Pale Quote remains untested. Chartreuse Punctuation was retained after a neutral physical response; it has not been promoted to an accepted preference.

Start with **Operator Ice**. It asks one bounded question: does one visibly separate punctuation group help when glancing down, without distracting from the green writing field? If it feels busy, Pale Quote is the smaller trial. Three-Role Split tests whether extra distinctions add anything useful. Chartreuse Punctuation asks whether one consistent punctuation color works better than Three-Role Split's three groups. These are design hypotheses, not claims of faster typing.

## Exact assignments

**Pale Quote:** QUOTE #C0FFFF.

**Operator Ice:** MINUS #FFFFFF; PLUS #FFFFFF; SLASH #FFFFFF; BACK_SLASH #FFFFFF.

**Three-Role Split:** MINUS #FFFFFF; PLUS #FFFFFF; SLASH #FFFFFF; BACK_SLASH #FFFFFF; QUOTE #00FFFF; SEMICOLON #A0FF00; COMMA #A0FF00; PERIOD #A0FF00.

**Chartreuse Punctuation:** MINUS, PLUS, BACK_SLASH, SEMICOLON, QUOTE, COMMA,
PERIOD and SLASH are all #A0FF00.

[Full Chartreuse Punctuation palette JSON](https://rana.github.io/inzone-profile-studio/data/studies/chartreuse-punctuation.json)
contains the exact full 81-key design. It uses the keeper colors as its base and
the chartreuse separator hue from Three-Role Split for all eight specified keys.
The blue tilde, mint brackets, green letters and Space, red Backspace/Delete/Esc,
modifiers, navigation and F banks stay at their keeper colors. This copies color
assignments, not another slot's native profile or settings. A neutral physical
response was recorded and the study was retained. Legend readability was not
separately confirmed.

MINUS is the -/_ key, PLUS is the =/+ key, BACK_SLASH is the backslash/pipe key. Each physical key has one light: its shifted and unshifted legends cannot be colored separately. The roles are mnemonic groupings, not an exhaustive model of Go or Markdown syntax.

## Compare safely

In the studio, open **Explore studies** and choose a named study; it opens **Compare** automatically. Alternatively, open a preview link, then choose **Compare**. An opened variation link defaults to its accepted **Amber Hinge + Rose (original)** reference. The app labels these as local variations, never as newly accepted palettes. Reloading or sharing the URL reconstructs the exact RGB assignments and recognizes the exact study name. Temporary previews preserve existing saved variations; **Leave preview** returns to the previous palette. Opening a preview does not change a connected keyboard.

Screen RGB is not calibrated LED output. Pale aqua may look white; chartreuse may blend toward green or the warm function row. A real trial needs a chosen slot, its fresh source export, backup, official Sony import/Save, fresh export verification and the user's seated-distance feedback. Keep the accepted slot available for comparison.

The first three studies were prepared with Claude Collaborator. Chartreuse Punctuation records an exact requested color assignment; model agreement or a preview cannot replace physical feedback. See [the research queue](RESEARCH.md) for the open question and stopping rule.
