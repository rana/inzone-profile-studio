# Frame studies

Frame studies explore a different relationship around the writing field while
keeping the accepted Amber Hinge + Rose reference intact. These are proposed
comparisons, not physically accepted profiles. Previewing a study does not change hardware;
a separate selected trial and fresh device evidence are required.

## Glacier Gates

[Preview Glacier Gates](https://rana.github.io/inzone-profile-studio/?profile=amber-hinge-rose&colors=TAB.FFFFFF%2CCAP.FFFFFF%2CENTER.FFFFFF).

**Question:** does a neutral command group add a useful distinction, or draw too
much attention from the green writing field?

Only three physical keys change from violet `#B000FF` to white `#FFFFFF`:

| Physical key | Requested RGB |
| --- | --- |
| Tab (`TAB`) | `#FFFFFF` |
| Caps Lock (`CAP`) | `#FFFFFF` |
| Enter (`ENTER`) | `#FFFFFF` |

The solid green letters, Space and punctuation remain unchanged. So do the mint
numbers and brackets, blue tilde and arrows, red stop/delete group, amber Win/Fn,
rose Shift pair, distinct Ctrl/Alt pairs, warm F-bank gradient and separate
Page Up/Page Down pair. All 81 keys remain illuminated at 100% brightness.

The hypothesis is that a neutral group can offer a different visual mood without
relearning the established colored landmarks. This is a design question, not a
claim about universal color psychology, speed or reduced fatigue. White could
instead dominate the field or feel too stark. Screen RGB cannot predict its
physical brightness or exact hue.

An earlier all-white look, Porcelain, was not preferred. Three white command keys
within a saturated palette ask a different question; that distinction does not
establish that this study will be liked. Keep Glacier Gates separate from the
white punctuation studies so a comparison can identify which change matters.

## Compare and keep the reference

Compare with the original Amber Hinge + Rose. The preview changes only the named
keys, and an exact study name does not confer acceptance. Save or share the
preview to keep it independently. A physical trial requires an explicitly chosen
slot, its fresh same-slot Sony export and backup, official import/Save, strict
fresh-export comparison and seated-distance feedback. Keep the accepted keeper
available. A palette JSON or preview link is not a native Sony import.
