# Frame studies

The latest physical feedback favored white Tab and Enter, requested that Caps
return to its previous violet, and liked the four white operator/path keys from
Operator Ice. The following revisions preserve those observations as design
inputs. The two-gate revision was saved and export-verified. The all-white
combination was also tried: white Tab felt out of place in that combined context,
and standalone Operator Ice was chosen for the moment. Glacier Syntax remains
an untested preview; no further combined hardware trial is currently selected.
Feedback on a parent does not automatically establish acceptance of its revisions.

The physical operator keys are `MINUS` (-/_), `PLUS` (=/+), `SLASH` (/?), and
`BACK_SLASH` (backslash/pipe). One light covers both shifted and unshifted legends.
All other 79 or 75 keys retain the keeper's exact colors, including the green
writing field, mint numbers/brackets, blue tilde/arrows, amber Win/Fn, rose Shifts,
Ctrl/Alt pairs, red stop/delete group, warm F banks and separate page-key pair.
All 81 keys remain illuminated at the requested 100% brightness.

## One useful comparison at a time

Glacier Gates removes the white Caps accent from the original three-key study.
Glacier Union combines the two white gates with the four white punctuation
landmarks. Its question is whether sharing white makes those six landmarks
useful together or crowds the green field.

Glacier Syntax keeps the liked gate pair white and changes only the four
operator keys from Glacier Union to azure. This holds one successful relationship
fixed while testing whether the two functional groups benefit from different
colors. Azure reuses the approved tilde hue rather than relying on a narrowly
separated pale aqua or amber shade. It intentionally also shares a hue with the
arrows; it does not create a color unique to operators.

Glacier Syntax remains a design hypothesis, and the two-gate revision has no
separate preference verdict yet. Azure operators may
appear less bright than white, may merge with other blue landmarks, or may prove
less useful than the shared-white version. Conversely, six white keys may draw
too much attention. Screen RGB cannot settle seated-distance readability,
physical hue or preference. There is no claim of improved typing performance or
universal color psychology.

## Preserved original

[Preview Glacier Gates Original](https://rana.github.io/inzone-profile-studio/?profile=amber-hinge-rose&colors=TAB.FFFFFF%2CCAP.FFFFFF%2CENTER.FFFFFF)
is the historical three-white-key design: `TAB`, `CAP` and `ENTER` are all
`#FFFFFF`. Its existing study ID remains `glacier-gates`, its exact color map is
preserved, and older links still identify that original comparison. The website
now labels it **Glacier Gates Original** and archives it; old native exports and
historical trial records keep their original identities. The current two-gate
revision uses the separate ID `glacier-gates-v2`.

## Physical trials

Previewing or downloading does not change a connected keyboard. These public
JSON files are full palette designs, not Sony native imports. A selected physical
trial requires the intended slot's fresh own export and backup, official Sony
import/Save, strict fresh-export comparison and separate physical feedback.
Preserve the accepted keeper for comparison, and retain the original trial's
rollback and identity. Do not infer current live slot state from these studies.
